Privacy Policy

Mealofy.ai (formerly Thali) · Effective 3 September 2026

Mealofy.ai is an AI-assisted meal planner for Indian households. This page explains what information the service collects, why, and what you can do about it. It applies to the website at thali-iota.vercel.app and to the Mealofy.ai mobile app.

1. Information you give us

  • Account details. Your email address. If you sign in with Google, we also receive the name and email address on your Google account.
  • Household profiles. Details you enter about the people you cook for: name, age or date of birth, sex, relationship to you, dietary identity, health conditions, religious alignment, cuisine preferences, likes and dislikes, and spice tolerance. Health and religious details are sensitive; entering them is your choice, and they are used only to decide which dishes are suitable.
  • Pet profiles. Species, breed, age and health notes for pets you add.
  • Kitchen data. Pantry items, meal plans, saved routines, shopping lists and favourites.

2. How we use it

To sign you in, to generate meal plans, recipes, cooking guidance and shopping lists that fit everyone at your table, and to fix problems with the service. We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

3. AI processing

To generate a meal plan, the dietary, health and preference details of the people eating are sent to Google's Gemini API, which returns dish suggestions. This is the core of how the service works. We do not send your email address or account credentials to the AI provider.

4. Where your data is stored

  • Website. Household profiles entered on the website are stored in your own browser on your device. They are sent to our server only while a meal plan is being generated.
  • Mobile app. Account and household data are stored in a Supabase database hosted in Mumbai, India, protected so that each account can only read its own household.

5. Services we rely on

Supabase (database and sign-in), Google (Google sign-in and the Gemini AI model), Vercel (website hosting) and Gmail (sending sign-in codes). Each receives only what it needs to do its job.

6. Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. Email mealofy.ai@gmail.com from your sign-in address to see, correct or delete it; deletion is completed within 30 days. Website data stored in your browser can be cleared at any time from the site itself or from your browser settings.

7. Children

The service is meant for adults. Profiles for children are created and controlled by the adult who holds the account.

8. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections and access is limited to what the service needs. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a sign-in method you trust.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will be published on this page.

10. Contact
